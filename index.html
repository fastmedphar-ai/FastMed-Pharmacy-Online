About Us

A community pharmacy for Ajuy and nearby barangays

FastMed Pharmacy serves residents of Ajuy and neighboring communities from our location on Marina Street in Poblacion. We aim to make everyday medicines and healthcare essentials easier to get, close to home.

Every visit is handled with care: we dispense medicines responsibly, take time to answer your questions, and help you understand how to take your medicines properly.

  • Accessible healthcare essentials

    Everyday medicines and wellness products within reach of the community.

  • Responsible medicine dispensing

    Prescription medicines are dispensed only with a valid prescription.

  • Customer-centered service

    Friendly, respectful help whether you are visiting, reserving, or asking a question.

  • Professional pharmaceutical care

    Guidance on proper use, storage, and timing of your medicines.

Services

Pharmacy services at FastMed Ajuy

Practical, everyday pharmacy services for individuals and families.

Prescription medicine dispensing

We fill prescriptions from your doctor and explain how to take your medicines.

A valid prescription is required for prescription-only medicines.

Over-the-counter medicines

Common non-prescription medicines for everyday concerns. Ask our staff if you are unsure what is right for you.

Vitamins and supplements

Vitamins, minerals, and supplements for you and your family.

Basic health and wellness essentials

Everyday health, hygiene, and first-aid items for the home.

Medicine availability inquiries

Ask us before you visit whether an item is in stock. Send an inquiry.

Guidance on proper medicine use

Clear explanations on dosage schedule, storage, and what to watch out for.

Delivery within Ajuy

We deliver orders within Ajuy. Message us to arrange a delivery.

Reservations via Messenger
